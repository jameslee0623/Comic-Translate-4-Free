## Free use

The core translation tool is free. Manual translation is free and unlimited
when all Pro features are turned off in Settings.

Auto-translate, pre-translate, and engine failover are Pro features. They
are free for 50 images a day. After 50 images in a day, those features stop
until the next day, unless you have an active Pro subscription. A long strip
counts as the comic pages it spans (a strip 3 pages long counts as 3, 5
pages long counts as 5), not as one image. Re-translating the same image
does not count again.

## Pro subscription

Pro unlocks unlimited auto-translate, pre-translate, and engine failover.

- Price: $3/month or $30/year.
- You buy a license key through the checkout link in the extension's
  Settings (Pro section), paste the key into the License key field, and
  click Activate Pro.
- One license key works on 2 browser installations.
- Your Pro access follows your subscription status. If the subscription
  ends or is cancelled, the key stops unlocking Pro.
- Do not share your license key. Keys found shared or abused may be
  revoked.
- Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record. The
  extension never sees or stores your payment details. To cancel, manage,
  or ask about a payment or refund, use the Dodo customer portal linked
  from your purchase confirmation email.
