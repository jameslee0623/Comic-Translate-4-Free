# Pricing — Comic Translate 4 Free

Last updated: November 25, 2026

## Free

- Manual translation is free and unlimited when all Pro features are
  turned off in Settings.
- Auto-translate and pre-translate are free for
  50 images a day. After 50 images in a day, those features stop until
  the next day. Engine failover is free, always on.
- One image counts as one, whatever its height. Re-translating the same image
  does not count again.

## Pro — $3/month or $30/year

Pro unlocks unlimited auto-translate and pre-translate.
No daily limit.

- Buy a license key at
  https://checkout.dodopayments.com/buy/pdc_0NshS3ePj2aiNr7Cj03d9
- Paste the key into the License key field in the extension's Settings
  (Pro section) and click Activate Pro.
- One key works on 2 browser installations.
- Your Pro access follows your subscription status. Cancel any time from
  the Dodo customer portal; the key stops unlocking Pro when the
  subscription ends.
- No lifetime plan. Subscriptions only.

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments. See `TERMS.md` for the full
terms and `PRIVACY.md` for what data Pro uses.
